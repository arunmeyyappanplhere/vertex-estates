Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

VERTEX Estates & Build ("VERTEX", "we", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of every visitor, client, and partner who engages with our platform and services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the safeguards we apply to keep it secure.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details — name, email address, and phone number submitted through inquiry forms, quote requests, or newsletter subscriptions.
  • Property preferences — listings viewed, saved searches, and filter selections used to improve our recommendations.
  • Technical data — browser type, device information, and anonymized analytics about how the site is navigated.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule viewings, and prepare custom quotes.
  • To deliver the quarterly Architectural Brief newsletter you have opted into.
  • To improve site performance, listing accuracy, and user experience.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Information is shared only with trusted service providers acting on our behalf (such as escrow, inspection, and CRM partners), and only to the extent necessary to deliver the services you requested — always under strict confidentiality terms.

4. Confidentiality Protocol

All client correspondence relating to off-market estates and institutional developments is held under NDA-grade protocol. Access to sensitive records is restricted to authorized personnel and retained only as long as operationally or legally required.

5. Your Rights

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link or by contacting us directly.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you in a portable format.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact our data protection team at contact@vertexestates.build or call +1 (800) 837-8390.
